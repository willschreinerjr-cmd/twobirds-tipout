Place your final app logo files in this folder with these exact names:

- apple-touch-icon-180.png  (180x180, for iPhone home screen)
- icon-192.png              (192x192, for Android/PWA)
- icon-512.png              (512x512, for Android/PWA)
- icon-maskable-512.png     (512x512, maskable safe area)

Design tips:
- Use a square icon with a transparent or solid background.
- Keep the main mark centered with padding so it isn't cropped.
- For maskable icon, keep important content inside the center 80%.

After replacing icons:
1) Refresh the site once.
2) Remove old app icon from iPhone home screen.
3) Add to Home Screen again.
